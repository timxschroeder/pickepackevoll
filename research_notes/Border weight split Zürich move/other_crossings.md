# Alternative German–Swiss crossings (not Basel) for splitting the van load before Zürich

Scope: Rheinfelden, Waldshut–Koblenz, Bad Zurzach–Rheinheim, Schaffhausen area (Thayngen/Bietingen + town crossings), Konstanz–Kreuzlingen. Research date 2026-10-14. Note: the old BAZG office-directory hosts (offices.customs.admin.ch, pwebapps.ezv.admin.ch) did not resolve when fetched; the current directory is https://dst.bazg.admin.ch/?lang=1 (JavaScript app, could not be read by the fetch tool). Hours below that cite the old directory URLs come from search-engine snippets of those official pages, so treat them as "official but possibly stale" and confirm by phone (BAZG Auskunftszentrale +41 58 467 15 15).

## 1. General rule: where and when household goods (Übersiedlungsgut) can be cleared

### Takeaway
Household goods are cleared only at offices handling commercial goods, Mon–Fri during goods hours, and at some large offices also on Saturday morning. Among the candidates, Kreuzlingen-Autobahn, Thayngen and Koblenz list Saturday 08:00–12:00. Rheinfelden-Autobahn is closed on Saturday. No goods office was confirmed at Zurzach.

### Cited Findings
- BAZG FAQ: household goods can in principle only be cleared Mon–Fri during the opening hours of the offices responsible for commercial goods ("bei gewissen grossen Zollstellen auch Samstag Vormittag") — [BAZG FAQ Umzugsgut](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/bazg/de/home/information-private/uebersiedlung--studium--feriendomizil--heirat-und-erbschaft/einfuhr-in-die-schweiz/umzug--uebersiedlungsgut-/faq-umzugsgut.html)
- BAZG: many small crossings are staffed only part-time, some not at all. Commercial goods declarations are generally possible Mon–Fri, with some offices also open on Saturday morning. Check each office's hours. Directory: dst.bazg.admin.ch — [BAZG Standorte & Öffnungszeiten](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/standorte-und-oeffnungszeiten-grenzuebergaenge)
- Auskunftszentrale +41 58 467 15 15, Mon–Fri 08:00–11:30 / 13:30–17:00; contact form kontakt-formular.bazg.admin.ch — [BAZG Standorte](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/standorte-und-oeffnungszeiten-grenzuebergaenge)
- BAZG Richtlinie 10-24 (in force since 1 Jan 2025) governs bringing goods over unstaffed customs roads or offices without clearance competence, under cumulative conditions (not read in full) — [BAZG Richtlinie 10-24 PDF](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/ZIkmrLj3gx5I/richtlinie10-24-de.pdf)

### Inferences
- A Saturday crossing only works at Kreuzlingen-Autobahn, Thayngen or Koblenz, and only before 12:00. Basel's large offices are likely comparable, but Basel is out of scope here.

### Gaps
- I could not read the live dst.bazg.admin.ch entries, so every office's hours need a phone check before the move date.

## 2. Rheinfelden (DE) – Rheinfelden AG (CH)

### Takeaway
The goods office is Rheinfelden-Autobahn (A3/A861 bridge). It is open weekdays only, with no Saturday clearance. Rental options in Rheinfelden AG are weak (local garages and brokers). A rental van from Basel would probably be easier. Suitability: poor for a Saturday, fair on a weekday.

### Cited Findings
- Swiss office Rheinfelden-Autobahn, Im Weiherfeld, Zollgebäude N3-A98, 4310 Rheinfelden. Goods import Mon–Fri 07:00–17:00, export Mon–Fri 07:30–18:00, Sat/Sun closed. The page lists household moves under these goods hours — [BAZG directory, Rheinfelden-Autobahn (old URL, via search snippet)](https://www.offices.customs.admin.ch/dst/print?id=65&lang=1)
- A third-party directory lists the town crossing Rheinfelden-Stadt (old Rhine bridge) at 0–24 h for the Swiss side. That probably means passenger traffic, not goods clearance (unverified) — [zolltarifnummern.de Rheinfelden](https://www.zolltarifnummern.de/zollamt/Rheinfelden%20(Baden))
- Rental: Garage Egli AG in Rheinfelden is listed for rental cars, but vans are not confirmed (tel. 061 836 87 87 per directory) — [miettransporter.ch Rheinfelden](https://www.miettransporter.ch/rheinfelden.html)
- Transportero advertises moving vans "Rheinfelden" (Renault Master with tail lift, from CHF 140 incl. 150 km and full insurance), but pickup appears to be in Aarau, about 25 min away — [transportero.ch Rheinfelden](https://transportero.ch/aargau/rheinfelden/)
- easy-transporter.ch advertises moving vans in Rheinfelden/Fricktal from CHF 80 (station and hours not checked) — [easy-transporter.ch](https://easy-transporter.ch/rheinfelden)
- Mobility car-sharing has locations "Rheinfelden" and "Rheinfelden Augarten". Whether they include vans was not verified — [SBB Mobility page](https://www.sbb.ch/de/bahnhof-services/velo-auto/auto-mieten/mobility.html)

### Inferences
- The old Rhine bridge between the two town centres is a pedestrian/town crossing (known locally). Rheinfelden AG station is a short walk from it, so the walk to the Swiss side is easy. Since rental stations are thin, Michelle would probably need to take the train to Basel (about 15 min) to rent. That removes most of the advantage over Basel.
- On the A5 route from Hamburg, Rheinfelden is a short detour past Basel via the A861/A3. Zürich is about 80 km away, roughly 1 h via the A3 (estimate, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No major brand (Europcar/Hertz/Sixt/Avis) station was confirmed in Rheinfelden AG.
- No source found for truck/van parking on the German side (Rheinfelden Baden).

## 3. Waldshut (DE) – Koblenz AG (CH)

### Takeaway
There is a staffed goods office with Saturday 08:00–12:00. You can walk across the Rhine bridge to Koblenz AG station. Rental options are local (lieferwagen-mieten.ch in Rekingen, 9 min drive). No big-brand station was found. Suitability: good for customs and walking, but rental logistics need a phone check.

### Cited Findings
- Swiss office Koblenz: goods Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat 08:00–12:00, Sun closed. Transit Mon–Fri 05:00–19:00. Household moves are named among the clearance services. A snippet puts the office at Georg-Wittig-Strasse 1, Waldshut-Tiengen, i.e. possibly a joint facility on the German side (unverified) — [BAZG directory, Koblenz (old URL, via search snippet)](https://www.offices.customs.admin.ch/dst/printmobile?id=122&lang=1&sprache=1)
- JuraLogistik (private) lists the Koblenz crossing at Rheinbrückerstrasse, 5322 Koblenz, Mon–Fri 07:30–17:30, Sat 08:00–12:00 — [juralog.ch Koblenz](https://www.juralog.ch/alle-unsere-zollstellen/koblenz-waldshut-tiengen)
- German side: Zollamt Waldshut is listed Mon–Fri 05:00–19:00, Sat 08:00–12:00 (directory, not official) — [zolltarifnummern.de Waldshut](https://www.zolltarifnummern.de/zollamt/DE004208)
- Rental: lieferwagen-mieten.ch station at Vortaue 5, 5332 Rekingen AG, near Rekingen station. The site says it is 9 min from Koblenz and 5 min from Bad Zurzach. Hours and vehicle sizes are not stated, tel. 0848 300 500 — [lieferwagen-mieten.ch Bad Zurzach](https://www.lieferwagen-mieten.ch/anfahrt/bad-zurzach.html)
- No Mobility location in Koblenz AG was found — [SBB Mobility page](https://www.sbb.ch/de/bahnhof-services/velo-auto/auto-mieten/mobility.html)

### Inferences
- Michelle could walk over the bridge to Koblenz AG station (about 1 km), take the train to Rekingen (one or two stops), pick up the van and drive back. That is about 45–60 min in total (estimate).
- The route from Hamburg via the A5 or A81 needs about 40–60 km of B-roads to Waldshut. Koblenz → Zürich is about 50 km, roughly 50 min (estimate, not sourced).

### Gaps
- Rekingen station hours, Saturday opening and one-way return in Zürich are unknown.
- No source found on parking a 3.5 t van in Waldshut near the bridge.

## 4. Bad Zurzach (CH) – Rheinheim (DE)

### Takeaway
I found no evidence of a staffed goods/household-goods office at this crossing. Treat it as unsuitable for customs. The nearest goods office is Koblenz, about 10 km away. Rental van at Rekingen (5 min) is the same as for Koblenz.

### Cited Findings
- In May 2020 (COVID period) BAZG reported the Bad Zurzach crossing fully closed to trucks and goods, open to persons only at certain times. This is old information but suggests a minor crossing — [BAZG press release 2020 PDF](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/i8ZtrI2vHlcn/20200505-mm-grenzoeffnung-bad-zurzach.pdf)
- Bad Zurzach station has P+Rail with 37 spaces. Mobility is not mentioned — [SBB Bahnhof Bad Zurzach](https://www.sbb.ch/de/bahnhof-services/am-bahnhof/bahnhoefe/bahnhof.3409.bad-zurzach.html)
- Rental: Rekingen station of lieferwagen-mieten.ch, 5 min from Bad Zurzach — [lieferwagen-mieten.ch](https://www.lieferwagen-mieten.ch/anfahrt/bad-zurzach.html)

### Inferences
- Any loaded van with household goods should declare at a goods office (Koblenz). Crossing at Zurzach would mean a detour or the Richtlinie 10-24 procedure, which is not meant for private moves.

### Gaps
- No current official status of the Zurzach office was found.

## 5. Schaffhausen area – Thayngen (CH) / Bietingen (DE), A81 → A4

### Takeaway
Thayngen is the main goods office on the A81/A4 corridor, and the A81 is the natural route from Hamburg. It is open Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30 and Sat 08:00–12:00. Rental: Schaffhausen town (about 10 km) has Hertz (Sat 08–12), Avis/Munot (Sat 07–12), Europcar/AMAG (weekdays only per directory) and Sixt Neuhausen. Thayngen is not walkable to a rental station, but there is a train from Thayngen to Schaffhausen. Suitability: very good on route. Best alternative if they drive A7/A81.

### Cited Findings
- Thayngen office: Zollstrasse 87, CH-8240 Thayngen, Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat 08:00–12:00, tel. +41 58 480 90 00, zoll.schaffhausen@bazg.admin.ch (private broker page) — [baerzoll.com Thayngen](https://baerzoll.com/verzollung-in-thayngen-bietingen/)
- Commercial goods Thayngen Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat 08:00–12:00. Transit Mon–Sat 05:00–22:00 — [Cippà Trasporti](https://www.cippatrasporti.ch/de/zollhandlungen/die-zollstelle-thayngen/)
- Hertz Schaffhausen, Gennersbrunnerstrasse 58: Mon–Fri 07:30–18:00, Sat 08:00–12:00, vans in fleet, half-day van rentals, free return outside hours. Van sizes and one-way not stated — [hertz.ch Schaffhausen](https://www.hertz.ch/p/autovermietung/schweiz/schaffhausen)
- Avis Schaffhausen (Munot Rental Cars GmbH), 8200 Schaffhausen, tel. +41 52 624 00 22: Mon–Fri 07:00–18:00, Sat 07:00–12:00. Zürich return stations include Gartenhofstrasse 17 and Zürich Airport (Parking 3) — [avis.com Schaffhausen](https://www.avis.com/pt/locations/ch/schaffhausen)

Europcar's own page offers cars and vans — [Europcar Schaffhausen AMAG](https://www.europcar.co.uk/en-gb/stations/switzerland/schaffhausen-amag); [oeffnungszeitenbuch.de](https://www.oeffnungszeitenbuch.de/filiale/Schaffhausen-Europcar%2520Autovermietung-537677T.html)
- Sixt: Wiesengrundstrasse 5, 8212 Neuhausen am Rheinfall (via aggregator; hours unconfirmed) — [kayak Schaffhausen](https://www.kayak.com/Schaffhausen-Cargo-van-Rentals.28199.cvn.ksp)

### Inferences
- Plan: park in Bietingen (DE) before the joint customs area. Michelle takes a bus or taxi to Thayngen station (about 2–3 km), then the train to Schaffhausen (about 10 min), picks up at Hertz or Avis, drives back via Thayngen and returns to Bietingen. Allow about 1.5–2 h (estimate). The rental van then crosses into CH with the Crafter, with both cleared at Thayngen.
- The Schaffhausen town crossings (e.g. Neuhausen, Bargen, Dörflingen) are small. No goods-clearance competence was found, so Thayngen is the one to use.
- Hamburg → Bietingen via A7/A81 is about 800 km (estimate). Thayngen → Zürich is about 50 km, roughly 45 min via A4 (estimate, not sourced).

### Gaps
- No source was found for parking at Bietingen (DE) for a loaded 3.5 t van. There are truck parking areas near the joint customs facility, but their status for vans is unverified.
- One-way to Zürich and Saturday van availability are not confirmed for Hertz or Avis Schaffhausen.

## 6. Konstanz (DE) – Kreuzlingen (CH)

### Takeaway
This is the best walkable crossing: the towns are contiguous, it is a short walk or a 3-min train ride, and Kreuzlingen-Autobahn clears household goods including Sat 08:00–12:00. Big brands are scarce (Europcar at Tägerwilen is weekdays only). Several local van renters in Kreuzlingen, Sixt is on the German side. Suitability: good, mainly limited by van rental hours on Saturday. Mostly a detour from the A81 via Singen.

### Cited Findings
- Kreuzlingen-Autobahn (Zoll Nordost): import/export Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat 08:00–12:00, Sun closed. Clearance times explicitly cover private special goods (moving, vehicles, marriage, inheritance) — [BAZG directory, Kreuzlingen-Autobahn (old URL, via search snippet)](https://www.offices.customs.admin.ch/dst/printmobile?id=174&sprache=1)

### Inferences
- From a parking spot in Konstanz near the Autobahn crossing (B33/A7 Kreuzlingen), Michelle could reach a Kreuzlingen renter on foot in 15–30 min (estimate).
- A local Kreuzlingen van is likely round-trip only. One-way to Zürich is unlikely, so someone would have to return it, or they would need Mobility or a brand station that allows one-way.

## 7. Comparison verdict (vs Basel, researched separately)

### Takeaway
Thayngen/Schaffhausen is the best non-Basel option if they drive via A7/A81: the goods office opens on Saturday morning and there are several brand stations with Saturday hours in Schaffhausen. Kreuzlingen is second: very walkable with Saturday customs, but weaker rental options. Koblenz has workable customs but thin rental options. Rheinfelden has no Saturday customs. Zurzach has no goods office.

### Cited Findings
- Saturday 08:00–12:00 goods clearance: Kreuzlingen-Autobahn, Thayngen, Koblenz. Rheinfelden-Autobahn is closed on Saturday — sources in sections 2–6 above.
- Saturday rental hours confirmed only for Hertz Schaffhausen (08–12) and Avis Schaffhausen (07–12) — [hertz.ch](https://www.hertz.ch/p/autovermietung/schweiz/schaffhausen); [avis.com](https://www.avis.com/pt/locations/ch/schaffhausen)

### Inferences
- On a Saturday, the window is tight: rental pickup from about 08:00 and customs closes at 12:00. A weekday (Friday) gives far more slack at every crossing.
- Every option relies on household goods being declared with form 18.44. Both vehicles should be cleared together, so the rental van's load must be on the same inventory list.

### Gaps
- All driving times and distances are estimates; no routing source was fetched.
- Whether brand rentals allow Swiss vans to drive into Germany and back, and one-way returns to Zürich for vans, was not verified.
