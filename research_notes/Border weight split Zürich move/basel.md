# Basel as the place to split the van load at the German–Swiss border (Option B)

Researched 2026-10-14. 17 tool calls in total. Several official pages (sixt.ch, the BAZG office-directory PDF) returned 403 or 502 errors, so some station details below come from third-party listings and are flagged as such. **Before relying on any address or opening hour, check it in the official station finder or by phone.**

## 1. Swiss rental stations in Basel offering class-B vans (≤3.5 t), with addresses, hours and distance from the border

### Takeaway
Europcar Basel offers vans from 2 to 20 m³, picked up in the city centre, at the airport or at the train station, and offers one-way rentals. The most convenient address for Option B is **Schwarzwaldallee 242** (Sixt and also Europcar, according to third-party listings). That is right next to Basel Badischer Bahnhof (Schwarzwaldallee 200), which is the first stop of the S-Bahn and regional trains from Weil am Rhein and Lörrach. Mobility car-sharing rules out one-way trips, so a Mobility van would have to go back to Basel. Hertz has city stations, but I could not confirm that they rent vans.

### Cited Findings
- **Europcar Basel vans:** "vans and trucks from 2 m³ to 20 m³", pick-up "city centre, Basel Airport, train station", and one-way rentals are available (no fees or conditions stated) — [Europcar Basel van rental](https://www.europcar.be/en-be/places/van-rental-switzerland/basel)
- Europcar rental requirements: a licence valid in the country of rental, ID, and a major credit card in the main driver's name. Debit and prepaid cards are not accepted — [Europcar Basel van rental](https://www.europcar.be/en-be/places/van-rental-switzerland/basel)
- Europcar's official "Basel Main Station" page says only that the office is "in Basel Main Station". It gives no street address, hours or phone number — [europcar.ch Basel Main Station](https://www.europcar.ch/en-ch/stations/switzerland/basel-main-station)
- Auto Europe (third party) lists the Europcar "Basel Rail Station" desk at **Schwarzwaldallee 242, 4058 Basel**, open Mon–Sat 07:30–18:30 and closed Sunday — [Auto Europe](https://www.autoeurope.ca/car-rental-basel-sbb-railway-station/) (UNVERIFIED on europcar.ch. Schwarzwaldallee is the street of the Badischer Bahnhof, not SBB, so the "Main Station" label may be mixed up.)
- **Sixt:** Kayak (third party) lists a Sixt branch at **Schwarzwaldallee 242**, open Saturday 07:30–23:00. It lists the EuroAirport branch as open Saturday 07:30–23:00 and Sunday 08:30–23:00 — via search-result summary of Kayak/aggregator pages ([search context](https://www.vn.kayak.com/Cho-cho-thue-o-to-gia-re-tai-Basel.7959.cars.ksp)). The official sixt.ch page returned HTTP 403. **UNVERIFIED. The 23:00 closing time looks unusual for a city branch.**
- **Basel Badischer Bahnhof** is run by Deutsche Bahn and sits at Schwarzwaldallee 200, 4016 Basel (Kleinbasel) — [bahnhof.de Basel Bad Bf](https://www.bahnhof.de/basel-bad-bf/mobilitaetsservice)
- **Hertz:** directory listings give Nauenstrasse 33, 4052 Basel (near SBB), phone +41 61 205 92 22. They also give Riehenstrasse 74 or 165 (the sources conflict). Hertz in Switzerland is run by the licensee Herold Fahrzeugvermietung AG. I found no confirmation that the Basel stations rent vans — [local.ch Basel car rentals](https://www.local.ch/de/q/basel/car-rentals), [jobup.ch Hertz Basel job ad](https://jobup.ch/en/jobs/detail/4a0a6f4e-b3fd-4bca-ad13-ba5c802ba8e7)
- **Mobility car-sharing:** trips abroad to listed countries, including Germany and France, are allowed and covered by Mobility's insurance. Foreign vignettes and tolls are the customer's cost. The FAQ mentions a Transporter category but gives no Transporter-specific rules — [Mobility FAQ](https://www.mobility.ch/de/faq)
  - The FAQ's "must return to the original location" conflicts with older press reports of Mobility one-way stations at Basel Bahnhof and Basel Flughafen (2018–2019) — [Blick](https://www.blick.ch/wirtschaft/kein-zurueckbringen-mehr-noetig-mobility-verdoppelt-ein-weg-standorte-id15258145.html), [moneycab](https://www.moneycab.com/?p=1001887). The current FAQ takes precedence.
- **Tram 8** runs from Basel to "Weil am Rhein, Bahnhof/Zentrum", roughly every 15 minutes in the timetable samples seen — [search.ch timetable Basel Neubad](https://search.ch/fahrplan/Basel,Neubad); about the cross-border tram — [bs.ch media release 2010](https://www.bs.ch/medienmitteilungen/bvd/2010-tram-8-grenzenlos-baustelle-kleinhueningeranlage-abgeschlossen)

### Inferences
- **Most practical choice:** Michelle takes the S-Bahn (S6 from Lörrach, or the train from Weil am Rhein) to Basel Bad. Bf, one stop. She walks a few minutes to Schwarzwaldallee 242 and drives the van back over the Weil/Lörrach border. Total for this leg: probably about 20–30 min (inference; train times not checked).
- If Mobility is used, the van has to come back to Basel after Zürich. That is about 2 × 85 km plus a train back, so a one-way rental from Europcar or Sixt is much better.
- At a Swiss rental counter, an EU class B licence is valid for vans ≤3.5 t (general knowledge; the counter page only says "valid in the country of rental").
- Swiss-registered rental vans usually come with the Swiss vignette. Cross-border trips to Germany normally need to be allowed in the contract. Confirm both when booking (not verified per company).

### Gaps
- Official street address and Saturday hours for Europcar, Sixt, Hertz, Avis/Budget and Enterprise city stations. The official pages were blocked or did not show them. Check with each company's station finder or by phone.
- Whether the Schwarzwaldallee 242 listing is really one building used by both Sixt and Europcar. It could be an aggregator mix-up.
- One-way fees from Basel to Zürich for vans.
- I did not research Avis/Budget, Enterprise city stations, Migros/Hertz "Transporter" offers, or local garages (AMAG, Hertz-Transporter, Kuhn etc.).
- Whether rental contracts allow taking the van to Germany and back.

## 2. EuroAirport Basel-Mulhouse (French soil, Swiss sector) as a rental option

### Takeaway
It is possible: the Swiss sector (Hall 4) has Sixt, Europcar, Enterprise/Alamo/National and others, and Sixt is reportedly open on Saturday. But it is poorly placed for Option B. The airport is on French soil northwest of Basel, while the Crafter waits on the German side to the northeast. The rental also has to come back to the **Swiss** sector, because a French-side return can trigger repatriation fees.

### Cited Findings
- Desk locations: the French sector desks are in Hall 1, Level 2, and the Swiss sector desks are in Hall 4, Level 2. Opening hours vary — [MySwissAlps forum](https://www.myswissalps.com/community/thread/do-hire-cars-from-basel-airport-include-a-vignette/)
- Cars rented on the French side may not have a Swiss vignette. Cars without one get directed aside at the border to buy one — [MySwissAlps forum](https://www.myswissalps.com/community/thread/do-hire-cars-from-basel-airport-include-a-vignette/)
- Enterprise: the vehicle must be returned on the Swiss side. A French-side return can bring repatriation costs charged to the renter — [Enterprise CH return directions Basel Airport](https://www.enterprise.ch/en/support/arrival-/-return-directions-basel-airport-)
- Companies listed in the Swiss sector include Alamo, Dollar, Thrifty, Sixt, Europcar and National. Hertz is listed on the French side — [airportrentals.com](https://www.airportrentals.com/switzerland/basel-airport-car-rental) (third party)
- Sixt EuroAirport, according to an aggregator: Saturday 07:30–23:00 and Sunday 08:30–23:00 — UNVERIFIED (see section 1).

### Inferences
- From Basel SBB, the airport is reached by bus 50 (not verified here). From the German side that means changing trains in Basel. It probably adds 45–60 min compared with the Badischer Bahnhof option.
- From the airport, Michelle would drive via the Swiss customs road into Basel, then through Basel to Weil/Lörrach. That means extra border crossings with an empty van (simple, but more time).
- The airport is only worth it if the city stations have no vans, or are closed at the needed time (e.g., Sunday).

### Gaps
- Current van availability at the airport, by company. Whether the Swiss-sector fleet includes vans ≤3.5 t.

## 3. Parking a loaded 3.5 t van for a few hours on the German side (Weil am Rhein, Lörrach, Grenzach-Wyhlen)

### Takeaway
I found no source confirming a P+R lot or van-friendly lot with known height limits at the tram 8 terminus or in Weil am Rhein. Multi-storey garages exist near the Dreiländerbrücke and the Rheincenter, but their clearance heights are not documented. A ground-level lot is needed for a Crafter, since a high-roof Crafter is about 2.6 m tall (general knowledge; check the actual van). A good fit is a large ground-level supermarket or retail lot, or the station area in Weil am Rhein or Lörrach. **This still needs on-site or Google Maps checking.**

### Cited Findings
- In 2006, Basel-Stadt considered co-funding a P+R at the Weil am Rhein tram terminus, on condition the spaces were used by public transport passengers. Current status unknown — [bs.ch media release 2006](https://www.bs.ch/medienmitteilungen/2006-grenzueberschreitendes-tram-regierungsrat-trifft-erste-vorentscheide)
- 2023 tram 8 extension planning (Läublinpark): parking along the edge of the road on the south side will be removed — [Weil am Rhein, track planning presentation](https://weil-am-rhein.de/site/weil-am-rhein-2020/get/params_E115635723/3230674/Pr%C3%A4sentation%20Trassenplanung.pdf)
- A 2023 Weil am Rhein citizens' workshop lists park and ride among the measures people want, which suggests there is no full P+R yet — [Zukunftswerkstatt results](https://www.Weil-am-Rhein.de/site/weil-am-rhein-2020/get/params_E653073624/3237775/Zukunftswerkstatt_Ergebnisse_231005.pdf)
- Rhein Center, Hauptstraße 435, 79576 Weil am Rhein: car park listed as open Mon–Sat 08:00–22:00. No clearance height given — [coparking.ch](https://coparking.ch/en/Germany/parkings/33099)
- Other garages nearby: Goldbeck Parking, Schusterinsel 11 — [coparking.ch](https://coparking.ch/en/Germany/parkings/49747); a surface lot on the B317 — [coparking.ch](https://coparking.ch/en/Germany/parkings/8610); APCOA underground garage Europaplatz 1 (560 spaces, entrance via Basler Straße) — [presseportal](https://www.presseportal.de/pm/9373/5333700)

### Inferences
- Avoid underground garages (APCOA, probably Goldbeck) because of the typical 2.0–2.2 m clearance. This is general knowledge and not verified per garage.
- Do not leave the loaded van unattended overnight. A few hours in daylight on a busy, open retail lot is the usual pragmatic choice. Tim can also simply stay with the van.
- A useful alternative is to park near Lörrach or Weil am Rhein station (short S-Bahn ride to Basel Bad. Bf). Tim waits with the van, so security is a non-issue.

### Gaps

- I did not research rest areas or truck stops (Raststätte Weil am Rhein, Autohof) on the A5 before the border.

## 4. Which customs office clears private household goods (Übersiedlungsgut, form 18.44) at Basel, and when

### Takeaway
Household goods must be declared at a Swiss customs office that handles commercial goods (Handelswaren), during its opening hours. In practice that means Mon–Fri, and only a few large offices also open on Saturday morning. **Basel/Weil am Rhein-Autobahn** (the A5/A2 motorway crossing) is open, according to the official BAZG address list, **Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30 and Sat 07:00–13:00**. That makes it the natural choice, and it fits a Saturday move only if both vans arrive well before 13:00. Smaller crossings such as Riehen and Lörrach-Stetten are not suitable for commercial-goods clearance.

### Cited Findings
- Form 18.44, "Erklärung/Abfertigungsantrag für Übersiedlungsgut", plus a list of the goods and proof of a home in Switzerland (rental contract). EU/EFTA citizens do not need a residence-permit assurance. Goods must have been in personal use for at least 6 months — [BAZG: Umzug in die Schweiz, Vorgehen](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/vorgehen-umzug-in-die-schweiz); [Form 18.44 PDF](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/dam/de/sd-web/lokVGdOnQG-l/18_44_uebersiedlungsgut_d.pdf)
- Clearance happens at an office responsible for commercial goods, during its opening hours. No advance notice is needed. "bei gewissen grossen Zollstellen auch Samstag Vormittag" — [BAZG FAQ Umzugsgut](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/faq-umzugsgut) (FAQ 1, FAQ 4)
- **Later shipments** must already be declared on a separate list at the first import (FAQ 10). Incomplete documents allow a provisional clearance against a deposit (FAQ 8). Trucks must use the commercial-goods lane (FAQ 5) — [BAZG FAQ Umzugsgut](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/faq-umzugsgut)
- Pre-checking of the dossier is offered only at the Ticino offices (Stabio, Vedeggio, Chiasso), not at Basel — [BAZG Vorgehen](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/vorgehen-umzug-in-die-schweiz)
- **Basel/Weil am Rhein Autobahn customs office**, Postfach 246, 4019 Basel: Mon–Fri 07:00–17:30, Sat 07:00–13:00. Phone +41 58 466 11 11, zoll.basel_nord@bazg.admin.ch. This comes from the official BAZG address list PDF (cited through a search summary; a direct fetch failed with HTTP 502; document date unknown) — [BAZG Zollstellen address list](https://bazg.admin.ch/dam/bazg/it/dokumente/stab/news/extern_adresse-zollstellen-alle-sprachen-def.pdf.download.pdf/extern_adresse-zollstellen-alle-sprachen-def.pdf)
- Holiday opening hours 2025/26 for Basel/Weil-Autobahn: closed or morning-only around Christmas and New Year — [BAZG holiday hours 2025/26](https://bazg.admin.ch/dam/bazg/de/dokumente/aktuell/oeffnungszeiten-weihnachten-neujahr-2024-2025.pdf.download.pdf/oeffnungszeiten-feiertage-2025-2026_d.pdf)
- Official, current office directory with hours: [dst.bazg.admin.ch](https://dst.bazg.admin.ch/?lang=1). Hotline +41 58 467 15 15 — [BAZG locations page](https://www.bazg.admin.ch/de/standorte-und-oeffnungszeiten-grenzuebergaenge)
- A private customs broker (Gerlach, Basel-Weil-Autobahn export) is open Saturday 07:30–11:30. This is the broker's own office, not the BAZG office — [Gerlach](https://gerlach-customs.com/de-de/standorte/basel-weil-autobahn-export/)
- Riehen-Weilstrasse (a minor crossing): only travellers' allowances, no commercial goods (2005 document, dated) — [Grosser Rat BS](https://grosserrat.bs.ch/dokumente/100196/000000196395.pdf)

### Inferences
- Both vehicles should cross together and declare one 18.44 with one complete list of goods, covering what is in the Crafter and in the Swiss rental. The rental is Swiss-registered, so the vehicle itself needs no customs treatment.
- Vans ≤3.5 t are not trucks. They would normally report to the Swiss customs building, or use the "goods to declare" route at the motorway plaza, rather than the truck lane. **The exact lane at Weil-Autobahn is not verified**, so ask at the booth or call +41 58 466 11 11 beforehand.
- Basel-Freiburgerstrasse and Basel Bad. Bahnhof: I could not verify their hours for commercial goods, so do not plan on them.
- **German export side:** private household goods leaving the EU normally need no German export declaration unless a VAT refund is claimed. This is general knowledge, not sourced here.
- Weekday, or Saturday before about 12:00, is strongly preferred. If the Saturday hours are confirmed, a late-morning Saturday is tight.

### Gaps
- Live confirmation of the 07:00–13:00 Saturday hours at dst.bazg.admin.ch (the directory was not fetched).
- The process for private household goods at the motorway plaza (where to stop, any separate van lane), and typical waiting times. I found no first-hand reports.
- Hours for Basel-Freiburgerstrasse, Basel Bad. Bahnhof, Riehen and Lörrach-Stetten.

## 5. Route and time needed for the whole Option B sequence in Basel

### Takeaway
Estimate: about 3.5–5 hours from parking the Crafter on the German side to both vans arriving in Zürich. Add about 1–1.5 h if the rental has to be returned in Basel, and more if the customs queue is long. This is an inference built from the findings above. Times are rough, not sourced timetables.

### Cited Findings
- No direct source found for the full sequence. The parts rely on the station locations (sections 1 and 3) and the customs hours (section 4).

### Inferences (rough sequence)
1. Park the Crafter in Weil am Rhein or Lörrach (ground-level lot). Michelle takes the S-Bahn or tram 8 to Basel Bad. Bf / Schwarzwaldallee: about 20–30 min.
2. Rental paperwork at Sixt or Europcar: about 20–30 min.
3. Drive the empty van back to the Crafter, crossing the border at Weil/Lörrach: about 15–25 min.
4. Move 100–300 kg into the second van: about 30–45 min.
5. Both vans to Basel/Weil-Autobahn customs and clearance with form 18.44: about 30–90 min (unknown queue).
6. Drive to Zürich, 85 km: about 1 h 15 min.
7. One-way return of the rental in Zürich: about 15–30 min. If the return must be in Basel instead, add about 1 h 15 min of driving and 1 h by train.

### Gaps
- Real S-Bahn times, one-way rental prices, and customs waiting times.
