# Swiss van rental policies for a short cross-border trip (CH → DE → CH → Zürich)

Research date: 2026-10-14. Several provider sites (sixt.ch, some europcar.ch/hornbach.ch pages) returned 403/404 to the fetch tool, so some points rest on search-result summaries of official pages and are flagged "(snippet only)". Nothing below is a confirmed quote for a specific date. Confirm any CHF amount with the station before booking.

## Is driving into Germany allowed? Cross-border fees or restrictions for vans?

### Takeaway
Europcar CH explicitly allows foreign trips with its vans as long as the van comes back to a Swiss station. Germany carries no surcharge (the fee applies only to HR/CZ/HU/SK/SI). Sixt and Avis allow it only if a paid "foreign travel" option is booked; without it Sixt voids the insurance. **Mobility does not let customers who live abroad drive outside Switzerland**, so Michelle cannot use Mobility for the Germany leg while her address is German.

### Cited Findings
- Europcar CH commercial vehicles: driving abroad is allowed, but the vehicle must be returned in Switzerland, and one-way rentals from CH to abroad are not allowed. A border-crossing fee applies only for Croatia, Czech Republic, Hungary, Slovakia and Slovenia, and can be added at online booking. — [Europcar CH Nutzfahrzeug mieten](https://www.europcar.ch/de-ch/p/nutzfahrzeug-mieten)
- Conflicting Europcar page: a business/"Schweiz Tourismus" page reportedly lists entry to HR, CZ, HU, IT, SK, SI and "non-EU countries" as forbidden. Germany is not on that list. It is unclear whether this page covers vans. (snippet only) — [europcar.ch/schweiz-tourismus](https://www.europcar.ch/schweiz-tourismus)
- Sixt CH: the customer must tell Sixt about the trip and book the "Auslandsfahrt" option. Breaking the foreign-travel conditions voids all insurance and liability limits. Countries are grouped into 4 zones, and the vehicle category can restrict which countries are allowed. (snippet only, page returned 403) — [Sixt CH help: Mit dem Mietwagen ins Ausland](https://www.sixt.ch/help-center/articles/mit-dem-sixt-mietwagen-aus-der-schweiz-ins-ausland-welche-laender-sind-erlaubt/)
- Avis CH: driving in other countries needs a cross-border option added at reservation for a "small surcharge". The amount is not stated. Allowed countries are those listed in the booking conditions, and Germany is not named on the page. — [Avis CH Auslandsfahrten](https://www.avis.ch/rund-um-avis/extras/cross-border-fee)
- Hertz CH van fleet page: says nothing about foreign travel. A Hertz "driving restrictions" T&C page exists but was not read. — [Hertz CH Vans](https://www.hertz.ch/rentacar/productservice/index.jsp?targetPage=CH_Vans.jsp); [Hertz rental terms (driving restrictions)](https://www.hertz.ch/rentacar/reservation/reviewmodifycancel/templates/rentalTerms.jsp?KEYWORD=DRIVINGRESTRICTIONS&EOAG=BCN)
- Mobility AGB §21: trips abroad are allowed only to countries covered by Mobility's insurance (the FAQ includes Germany). **§21 lit. e: customers living outside Switzerland may drive only "innerhalb der Schweiz".** — [Mobility AGB](https://www.mobility.ch/de/agb); [Mobility FAQ](https://www.mobility.ch/de/faq)
- Mobility AGB §12 lit. e: bulky goods may only be carried in "Transport" category vehicles, not in passenger cars. — [Mobility AGB](https://www.mobility.ch/de/agb)
- Enterprise CH markets its Basel locations (EuroAirport and a delivery/collection-only point at Bahnplatz 1) for trips near the German/French border. The search found no van-specific foreign-travel terms. (snippet only) — [Enterprise CH Basel delivery](https://www.enterprise.ch/en/basel-delivery)

### Inferences
- Europcar CH looks like the cleanest fit: the T&C text explicitly allows Germany with no surcharge, and the van returns to Switzerland.
- With Sixt or Avis, the cross-border option must be booked. Otherwise an accident in Germany would be uninsured (Sixt says this explicitly).
- Mobility is ruled out for the Germany leg while Michelle's address is in Germany. It would work only if a Swiss-resident member (e.g. Tim, once registered in CH) books and drives. Even then it is round-trip only (see the next question).
- At the EuroAirport (Basel-Mulhouse), make sure the rental is from the Swiss sector (a CH-plated vehicle). The French-sector fleet is French-plated and has different terms.

### Gaps
- Exact Sixt and Avis cross-border fees in CHF for Germany, and whether Sixt vans (LKW category) fall into a zone that includes Germany. The pages were blocked or gave no amounts.
- Hertz CH and Enterprise CH van foreign-travel rules were not verified.
- Baumarkt van rentals in Switzerland (Jumbo, Hornbach CH, Coop Bau+Hobby, OBI/Migros do it+Garden): no Swiss terms or prices found, and the hornbach.ch page returned 404. German Baumarkt prices found in the search (e.g. OBI DE €19.99/h) do not apply to Switzerland. Baumarkt rentals are usually local, short-hour and domestic-only. Treat them as unknown.
- Local garages: no data. These need to be asked individually.

## One-way (e.g. Basel → Zürich) for vans, and the fee

### Takeaway
Europcar CH allows one-way van rentals between some Swiss stations, Monday to Thursday only, so not on a Saturday. Avis advertises no one-way fee between Basel, Bern, Geneva, Lausanne and Zürich, but that may be for cars only. Sixt offers one-way for vans "possibly with a fee". Mobility does not allow one-way.

### Cited Findings
- Europcar CH vans: one-way is possible between certain Swiss stations **only Monday to Thursday**. The fee is not stated. — [Europcar CH Nutzfahrzeug mieten](https://www.europcar.ch/de-ch/p/nutzfahrzeug-mieten)
- Avis CH: return at any Avis station in CH depending on the rate type, and no one-way fee between Basel, Bern, Genève, Lausanne and Zürich. (snippet only) It is not clear whether this covers vans. — [Avis CH Einwegmiete](https://www.avis.ch/rund-um-avis/serviceleistungen/einwegmiete)
- Sixt CH van pages (Zürich, Basel, etc.) say one-way is offered but "a fee may apply", and the fee is shown before confirmation. (snippet only) — [Sixt CH Lieferwagen Basel](https://www.sixt.ch/lkw/schweiz/basel/)
- Mobility: vehicles must be returned to the original location. — [Mobility FAQ](https://www.mobility.ch/de/faq)
- Hertz: one-way depends on station and vehicle type, and the fee is shown at reservation. (snippet only) — [Hertz Einwegmieten](https://www.hertz.ch/rentacar/productservice/index.jsp?targetPage=caonewayrentals.jsp)

### Inferences
- A Saturday one-way Basel → Zürich with a Europcar van is not offered under the published rule. Options are a weekday, a round trip (return to Basel), or Avis/Sixt with a quoted one-way fee.
- If the load is only 100–300 kg, a large estate car one-way (Avis, Basel → Zürich, no fee if the rate type allows) may be the simplest option, provided the foreign-travel option covers Germany.

### Gaps
- One-way fee amounts in CHF for vans at Sixt, Hertz and Avis.

## Typical price, deposit, credit card, minimum age, licence held, foreign licence/address

### Takeaway
Rate structures are known (Europcar: night up to 4 h, half-day up to 5 h, 24 h day, 150 km/day included; Hertz: 150 km included). Concrete CHF prices could not be retrieved from official pages. Minimum age is 19 at Hertz vans, and Avis says 21 + ½ year licence for most vehicles. A category B licence is enough for all Europcar CH vans.

### Cited Findings
- Europcar CH van rates: night up to 4 h (18:00–07:00), half-day up to 5 h, full day 24 h, long-term over 30 days. 150 km included per rental day, with more km bookable. Category B suffices for all vans. A separate "Kautionsrichtlinie" covers the deposit (not read). — [Europcar CH Nutzfahrzeug mieten](https://www.europcar.ch/de-ch/p/nutzfahrzeug-mieten)
- Hertz CH vans: minimum age 19 and 150 km included. Theft/damage insurance is included, no credit-card fee, return outside opening hours is possible, reservations on 0848 822 020 (Mon–Sun 08–18). Prices are not shown. — [Hertz CH Vans](https://www.hertz.ch/rentacar/productservice/index.jsp?targetPage=CH_Vans.jsp); [Hertz Transporter mieten](https://www.hertz.ch/p/transporter-mieten/schweiz)
- Avis CH: most vehicles require age 21 and a full licence held for at least ½ year. Some groups require 25, and other Avis pages say 19 for many groups, so the sources conflict. (snippet only) — [Avis CH unter 25](https://www.avis.ch/mietwagen-f%C3%BCr-unter-25-j%C3%A4hrige)
- Sixt CH vans: a licence held at least 1 year, depending on category. (snippet only) — [Sixt CH Lieferwagen Zürich](https://www.sixt.ch/lkw/schweiz/zuerich/)
- Mobility: no fixed minimum age beyond 18 (with guardian under 18). Drivers under 28 and drivers with a licence under 3 years pay +CHF 500 deductible each (capped at +1,000). Transport category deductible is CHF 3,000 (max CHF 4,000 per claim). — [Mobility AGB](https://www.mobility.ch/de/agb)
- Europcar Funway customer cards are only for Swiss residents. This does not concern normal rentals. — [Europcar Funway conditions](https://www.europcar.ch/Bedingungen_Funway_Kundenkarten)

### Inferences
- An EU/German category B licence is accepted for renting in Switzerland by the major brands (standard practice). Not verified from a CH T&C text, so treat as likely.
- A credit card in the driver's name is normally required for the deposit at Europcar, Sixt, Hertz and Avis. Not verified for CH vans.

## Customs: German resident driving a Swiss-plated rental into Germany and back

### Takeaway
On the EU side this is explicitly allowed. Since an amendment to the EU UCC Delegated Regulation, a natural person resident in the EU may use a non-EU-registered road vehicle rented from a professional rental company under a written contract for private use, with full duty relief, as long as the vehicle leaves the EU again **within 8 days** (Art. 215(2a) + 218(4) UCC-DA). A few-km trip into Germany and back the same day is fine. On the Swiss side, a CH-registered rental returning to Switzerland is a domestic vehicle. The household goods are a separate customs matter.

### Cited Findings
- Art. 215(2a) Reg. (EU) 2015/2446: persons with habitual residence in the EU get full relief from import duty for road vehicles rented under a written contract with a professional car-rental company for private use. — [Art. 215 VO (EU) 2015/2446 (gesetze.legal)](https://gesetze.legal/eu/vo_eu_2015_2446/215); [thetradehub Art. 215](https://www.thetradehub.eu/en/reglementation/uk/eu/da-2446-2015/article/215)
- Art. 218(4) Reg. (EU) 2015/2446: in cases under Art. 215(2a), the road vehicle must be re-exported within 8 days of being placed under temporary admission. — [Art. 218 VO (EU) 2015/2446](https://gesetze.legal/eu/vo_eu_2015_2446/218)
- Older guidance (ÖAMTC, Austrian BMF) says an EU resident could use a third-country rental only to return home and had to hand it to a rental branch immediately, and that otherwise import duties apply. This predates or ignores the (2a) amendment. — [ÖAMTC Schutzbrief info](https://www.oeamtc.at/schutzbrief_info/72/); [BMF AT Firmen-KFZ](https://www.bmf.gv.at/zoll/fuer-unternehmen/RegelungFirmenfahrzeuge/Firmen-KFZ_in_der_EU.html)
- The rental company can still restrict this. Mobility forbids driving abroad for customers living abroad. — [Mobility AGB §21 e](https://www.mobility.ch/de/agb)

### Inferences
- The current consolidated text (2a + 8-day limit) is the applicable rule. The ÖAMTC wording is outdated or refers to the older Art. 215(2) case. Carry the written rental contract at the border.
- After Michelle registers as a Swiss resident, the EU rule no longer matters for her (non-EU resident with a non-EU vehicle → Art. 212 standard temporary admission). The rental's own T&Cs then govern.
- Swiss side: a Swiss-registered vehicle re-entering CH needs no vehicle formalities. Declare the goods carried (household goods, Übersiedlungsgut form 18.44, etc.) at a manned customs office during opening hours. That falls outside this rental-focused note.
- Carrying her own household goods in the rental is ordinary private use, not commercial transport. No provider T&C read prohibits it. Mobility explicitly requires a Transport-category vehicle for bulky goods.

### Gaps
- No BAZG page was found that specifically addresses a non-resident driving a Swiss-plated rental out of and back into CH. It is assumed unproblematic but not verified.
- Whether the 8-day limit was amended after 2024 (verify on EUR-Lex consolidated text).

## Payload of typical rental van classes; is an estate car enough for 100–300 kg?

### Takeaway
Every van class offered in Switzerland has a payload far above 300 kg (Europcar: 621 kg Caddy Maxi up to 1,250 kg Iveco Daily). The weight is a non-issue for any van. A large estate car would also carry 300 kg by weight; the limit there is volume.

### Cited Findings
- Europcar CH payloads: VW Caddy Maxi 621 kg, VW T6 Transporter 904 kg, VW Crafter short 1,131 kg, Iveco Daily short 1,250 kg, VW Crafter long 960 kg, VW Crafter with tail lift 750 kg. — [Europcar CH Nutzfahrzeug mieten](https://www.europcar.ch/de-ch/p/nutzfahrzeug-mieten)
- Hertz CH vans: Vito 6 m³ (950 kg), Ford Transit 7 m³ (1,084 kg), Fiat Ducato 11 m³ (1,320 kg), Iveco Daily with tail lift 10.5 m³ (860 kg). Hertz's wording is ambiguous ("permitted total weight… empty weight plus payload"), but the figures look like payloads. — [Hertz CH Vans](https://www.hertz.ch/rentacar/productservice/index.jsp?targetPage=CH_Vans.jsp)

### Inferences
- 100–300 kg fits even the smallest van (Caddy Maxi, 621 kg). A T6/Transit-size van is plenty.
- Large estate cars typically have about 500–650 kg payload including occupants (general knowledge, not sourced). With 2 adults (~150 kg) plus 300 kg, that is borderline but usually OK. Check the specific car's registration (Fahrzeugausweis) payload.
- Mobility rule: bulky goods only in Transport-category vehicles, not in passenger cars. This matters if Mobility is used domestically.

### Gaps
- Estate car payloads from a cited source.

## Booking lead time / Saturday availability

### Takeaway
No official lead-time data was found. Europcar's one-way restriction (Mon–Thu) is the main Saturday constraint. Saturday is peak day for moving vans, so book early.

### Cited Findings
- Europcar CH one-way for vans only Mon–Thu. — [Europcar CH Nutzfahrzeug mieten](https://www.europcar.ch/de-ch/p/nutzfahrzeug-mieten)
- Hertz CH vans: return outside opening hours possible; reservation line open Mon–Sun 08–18. — [Hertz Transporter mieten](https://www.hertz.ch/p/transporter-mieten/schweiz)

### Inferences
- Saturday plan options: (a) a round-trip van from a Basel station (Europcar/Hertz/Sixt), returned in Basel; or (b) a Sixt/Avis one-way with a quoted fee; or (c) a weekday one-way with Europcar.
- Check Saturday opening hours of the specific Basel/Schaffhausen/Kreuzlingen stations. Many city stations close around midday on Saturday (assumption, not verified).
